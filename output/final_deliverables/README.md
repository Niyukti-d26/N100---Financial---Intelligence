# Features

## 1. ETL Pipeline

The ETL pipeline processes financial datasets and prepares them for analysis.

### Supported datasets

- Companies
- Profit & Loss Statements
- Balance Sheets
- Cash Flow Statements
- Financial Ratios
- Market Capitalization
- Stock Prices
- Peer Groups
- Documents
- Pros and Cons

### ETL Capabilities

- Data ingestion
- Data normalization
- Schema validation
- Data quality checks
- Database loading

# 2. Financial KPI Engine

The platform calculates more than **30 financial indicators**.

## Profitability Metrics

- Return on Equity (ROE)
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)
- Return on Assets (ROA)
- Net Profit Margin
- Operating Profit Margin

## Growth Metrics

- Revenue CAGR
- PAT CAGR
- EPS CAGR

## Financial Strength Metrics

- Debt to Equity Ratio
- Interest Coverage Ratio
- Free Cash Flow
- CFO Quality Score

## Valuation Metrics

- PE Ratio
- PB Ratio
- Dividend Yield
- Market Capitalization

---

# 3. Investment Screener

The screener allows analysts to identify companies based on financial conditions.

## Available Screening Strategies

### Quality Compounder

Identifies companies with:

- Strong profitability
- High ROE and ROCE
- Sustainable growth
- Healthy cash generation


### Value Pick

Focuses on:

- Attractive valuation
- Reasonable PE/PB ratios
- Strong fundamentals


### Growth Accelerator

Identifies:

- High revenue growth
- Increasing profitability
- Earnings momentum


### Dividend Champion

Finds:

- Stable dividend-paying companies
- Consistent payout history


### Debt Free Blue Chip

Finds:

- Low debt companies
- Strong balance sheets
- Large-cap businesses


### Turnaround Watch

Detects:

- Improving financial performance
- Recovery opportunities
